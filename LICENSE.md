# Licences

Copyright 2026 OlofLoov. Three licences cover this repository, by kind of
file. The full texts are in `LICENSES/`.

| What | Where | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware: schematic, board, footprints, symbols, the layout scripts that generate them, the enclosure | `hardware/` | CERN-OHL-P-2.0 |
| Software: firmware, scripts, CI and hooks | `firmware/`, `scripts/`, `.github/`, `.claude/hooks/` | MIT |
| Documents: specs, design notes, drawings, bench logs | `openspec/`, `docs/`, `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md` | CC-BY-4.0 |

A file that states its own licence keeps it. The ones that are not
OlofLoov's work:

- `hardware/pcb/lib/models/kicad/`: 3D models from the KiCad library,
  CC-BY-SA 4.0 with KiCad's exception for designs that use them. See the
  README there. The KiCad footprints and symbols embedded in the board
  and schematic files are under the same terms.
- The symbols and footprints of U1 (Raytac AN54LQ-15) and U2 (Vishay
  TSMP96000) in `hardware/pcb/lib/` started as imports from LCSC's
  EasyEDA library, which states no licence, and were then corrected
  against the makers' drawings. What they hold is the pad positions and
  pin names from the datasheets. CERN-OHL-P covers the corrections, not
  LCSC's part of them.
- `.claude/skills/no-ai-slop/`: see its `LICENSE`.
- `.claude/skills/openspec-*/`: generated by the OpenSpec CLI, MIT.

Datasheets are not in the repository. `docs/datasheets/manifest.tsv`
lists where each one comes from, and they stay their makers' property.

CERN-OHL-P asks anyone who passes the hardware on to keep these notices.
The source location for that purpose is
https://github.com/OlofLoov/antuna-remote-controller.
